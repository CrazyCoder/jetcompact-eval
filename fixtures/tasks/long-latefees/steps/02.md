Background for later; no code now, just acknowledge it. Finance decided how late fees work: an invoice is due 28 days after its date, and paying on the due date is on time. After that the fee is 129 basis points of the invoice total for each started 30-day period past the due date, for at most 5 periods, rounded like tax (SPEC 3.2). Customers in the eastern region pay no late fees.
